Be application-ready

Documents commonly requested

Keep these handy to speed things up. Lenders may ask for more depending on your case.

KYC

  • PAN card
  • Aadhaar / passport / voter ID
  • Current address proof
  • Recent photograph

Income

  • Salaried: last 3 months' salary slips
  • Last 6 months' bank statements
  • Form 16 or latest ITR
  • Self-employed: 2 years' ITR with computation

KYC

  • PAN and Aadhaar of promoters / partners
  • Business PAN
  • GST registration / Udyam / shop licence
  • Partnership deed or MOA & AOA, if applicable

Financials

  • Last 12 months' business bank statements
  • GST returns for the last 12 months
  • 2 years' ITR with balance sheet and P&L
  • Details of existing loans, if any

KYC & income

  • PAN, Aadhaar and address proof of all owners
  • Income proof: salary slips or ITR
  • Last 12 months' bank statements
  • Business proof, if self-employed

Property

  • Title deed and previous chain documents
  • Approved building plan
  • Latest property tax receipt
  • Encumbrance certificate

KYC & income

  • PAN, Aadhaar and address proof of applicants
  • Salary slips and Form 16, or 2–3 years' ITR
  • Last 6 months' bank statements
  • Proof of own contribution paid

Property

  • Sale agreement or allotment letter
  • Builder NOC and approved plan
  • Title documents and chain
  • Balance transfer: loan statement and sanction letter

Frequently asked questions

Does submitting an enquiry guarantee loan approval?

No. An enquiry is only a request for assistance. Final eligibility, sanction, pricing and disbursal are decided solely by the lender after its checks.

Which loans can Credislay help me with?

We assist with Personal Loans, Business Loans, Loans Against Property and Home Loans, including home loan balance transfers and top-ups.

What is the difference between a Loan Against Property and a Home Loan?

A Home Loan is used to buy or build a home, and that home is the security. A Loan Against Property lets you borrow against a property you already own, for almost any business or personal purpose.

Which documents may be required?

It depends on the loan and lender. Common documents include PAN, identity and address proof, bank statements, income proof, ITR, GST returns, business financials and, for secured loans, property papers. See the checklist above.

Can self-employed applicants enquire?

Yes. Self-employed individuals and business owners can enquire for any of our loans. Lenders may assess business vintage, turnover, banking, tax filings and financial performance.

Does Credislay charge a fee?

Please confirm any applicable service fee directly with Credislay before proceeding. Never pay an individual or intermediary without verifying the official terms.

Will enquiring affect my credit score?

No. Submitting an enquiry on this website does not access your credit report. A lender's credit bureau check, made later with your consent, may appear on your credit report.
