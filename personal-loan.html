Eligibility and documents

Typical requirements across lenders. Exact criteria depend on the lender and your profile.

Who can apply

  • Age typically 21 – 60 years
  • Salaried with a stable income, or self-employed with ITR
  • Indian resident
  • A healthy credit history (many lenders prefer a score of 700+)
  • Existing EMIs within the lender's income limits

Documents commonly requested

  • PAN card and Aadhaar
  • Current address proof
  • Salaried: last 3 months' salary slips
  • Last 6 months' bank statements
  • Form 16 or latest ITR
  • Self-employed: 2 years' ITR with computation

FAQs

Personal Loan questions, answered

How much Personal Loan can I get?

It depends on your income, credit score, existing EMIs and the lender's policy. Lenders usually keep your total EMIs within a set share of your monthly income.

Do I need collateral or a guarantor?

No. A Personal Loan is unsecured. Some lenders may ask for a co-applicant if your income or credit history is limited.

Can self-employed people apply?

Yes. Self-employed professionals and business owners can apply with ITRs, bank statements and business proof.

Can I repay early?

Many lenders allow part-prepayment or foreclosure after a minimum period. Charges, if any, vary by lender and are shared before you sign.

Will enquiring affect my credit score?

No. Submitting this form does not check your credit report. A lender may run a credit check later, with your consent.
