Are you eligible?

Eligibility and documents

Typical requirements across lenders. Exact criteria depend on the lender and your profile.

Who can apply

  • Business running for typically 2+ years (some lenders consider 1+)
  • Proprietors, partnerships, LLPs, private limited companies and professionals
  • ITR filed, with stable or growing turnover
  • GST registration preferred
  • Healthy credit history of the business and promoters

Documents commonly requested

  • PAN and Aadhaar of promoters / partners
  • Business PAN and GST / Udyam registration
  • Last 12 months' business bank statements
  • GST returns for the last 12 months
  • 2 years' ITR with balance sheet and P&L
  • Partnership deed or MOA & AOA, if applicable

FAQs

Business Loan questions, answered

Can I get a Business Loan without collateral?

Yes, many lenders offer unsecured business loans to eligible businesses. Larger amounts or newer businesses may need security, such as property.

Is GST registration mandatory?

Not always, but most lenders prefer it. Without GST, lenders rely more on bank statements and ITRs.

My business is less than 2 years old. Can I apply?

Some lenders consider businesses running for a year or more, usually with strong banking and a good promoter credit history. Share your details and we will check options.

How is the loan amount decided?

Lenders look at your turnover, profits, banking, existing loans and credit score to decide the amount and rate.

Will enquiring affect my credit score?

No. Submitting this form does not check your credit report. A lender may run a credit check later, with your consent.
